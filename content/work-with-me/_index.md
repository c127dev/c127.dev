I'm available for remote contract work.

## What I do

- **OpenWrt board bring-up and support** - from initial port through upstream
  submission
- **Linux network driver development** - MAC, PHY, and switch drivers
- **Upstreaming** - preparing patches and carrying them through review on the
  kernel mailing lists
- **Kernel debugging** - hard-to-reproduce failures, data path issues,
  hardware/software integration problems
- **Embedded firmware development**
- **Hardware-level diagnosis** - circuit analysis, component-level repair and
  modification

I write about this work in detail on [the blog](/posts).

## Who this fits

- Companies that build or integrate networking hardware
- Teams that need upstream support for their silicon
- Projects where the problem sits exactly between the hardware and the
  kernel - the point where most teams run out of people who can look at both
  sides

## How I work

Remote, on contract. One-off projects or ongoing collaboration. Timezone
flexible.
